Back

Saprophytic is a back-pocket game idea that I’ve been developing between classes for the past four years. It’s a narrative that follows three prisoners who were leased out to do high-risk maintenance work grappling with the loss of their original human bodies and facing a potentially sentient being that seems set on tearing them apart.

As it is, the game's too big for what I can do as one College Student. It deserves a bigger scope than what I can give it. I've been making other things in the meantime, but every once in a while, when the inspiration is too strong, I dip back in. Here’s a bunch of little parts of it I’ve finished

Concept Art

Aesthetically, I’ve leaned towards the game having a sharp, ps1-esqueaesthetic with an emphasis on moody red lighting. I want the facility to feel messy and dangerous, and I want the edgiest visual aspects to be unrealistic enough that they aren't disgusting.


Short Narrative Games

I’ve made a few games in the space of the universe, namely ‘Vault’ and ‘Terminal’

Vault is a short text exploration of characters entering the facility for the first time, coming to terms with their new bodies. It was made in Ink


Terminal is a simulated computer operating system that allows players to read through the emails of the main antagonist. Though it's all faked in Unity, the computer is a systems-driven design that allows for loading of custom 'programs' into memory and executing commands from them.



Music

For Saprophytic's music I hope to use folk instruments with a darker electric edge, paired with classical music to represent the facility's original owners.
