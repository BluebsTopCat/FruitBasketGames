GODSEND is an in-development video game about envoys of the Gods fighting in the wreckage of the old world about who gets to make the next one. It’s a hyper-stylized action narrative game that is turning out really cool, thank you very much.

In GODSEND, you play as the BLADE, the envoy of the god of struggle. Any bit of damage is enough to kill you instantly.

The world ended three days ago. You’re fifty miles outside of Las Vegas. Be the last one standing.

The game is still in an early development phase, so I can’t show much– but here’s a few video, screenshots, and music tracks that show off what it’s all about!

Gameplay

Designing this game, I wanted to make something tough-as-nails and strange. There are a lot of action games on the market, but few require perfect precision. In GODSEND, the player dies in one hit.

Visuals

I wanted to create a visual style that I could iterate on quickly, and hit a consistent style across the board on. Because of this, I decided to go with a black/white blocky aesthetic with large fills and perfect shadows. This means that characters are more immediately readable on top, and helps blend in graphic and 2d assets!




Music


For writing my music, I wanted to hit something grungy and strange, with an emphasis on sampled instruments. Alt-rock, or something in that area. I’ve also been working on creating entirely adaptive music in FMOD – sections of the track switch depending on if the player is in combat, or different regions.
